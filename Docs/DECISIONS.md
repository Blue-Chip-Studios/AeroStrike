# AeroStrike: decision log

Significant decisions made during BCDK-Build-Game, newest last. "Dev" = developer decided; "BCDK" = autonomous recommendation (reason given).

| # | Date | Decision | By | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2026-11-18 | Art style: **Stylized** (sleek military-aviation flavour of it). | Dev (brief) | Stated explicitly in the brief. |
| 2 | 2026-11-18 | Use **Blender 5.3** instead of the 5.2 the pre-flight expects. | Dev | 5.3 installed; official Blender MCP v1.0.3 add-on verified working on port 9876. Checker only accepts 5.2. |
| 3 | 2026-11-18 | Build fresh in this repo; no prior codebase. | Dev | Project created 2026-11-18. |
| 4 | 2026-11-18 | PvP structure: **free-for-all from one shared neutral carrier, with Wingman squads** (no fixed teams). | Dev | Best fit for "zero lobbies", Ace Bounty and friend squads. |
| 5 | 2026-11-18 | UI references in `Docs/UI/References/` (brief said `Docs/UI-refs/`). 5 are screenshots of another Roblox game (Tiny Skies Fighter) and 2 are AI concept renders. They are used for **layout and information hierarchy only**. AeroStrike gets its own visual identity, not a copy of that game's look. | BCDK | Originality; the brief names Tiny Skies as a feel reference, not a template. |
| 6 | 2026-11-18 | Original jet names (no real-world aircraft names): Sparrow T-2 (trainer), Viper IX (interceptor), Rhino HX (strike fighter), Wraith-X (premium stealth). | BCDK | Avoids trademark issues; names were listed as flexible. |
| 7 | 2026-11-18 | Steering: **follow-reticle "aim to fly"** on every platform (touch-drag / mouse / right stick move an aim point; the jet chases it). Manual roll is optional (advanced). | BCDK | Delivers "ace in 10 seconds" on mobile while keeping PC/console precise. |
| 8 | 2026-11-18 | Network model: client owns its own jet's physics for responsiveness; server validates speed, position deltas, fire rate and hit claims. Missiles are server-authoritative, with client-interpolated visuals. | BCDK | Responsiveness without trusting the client for damage, kills or rewards. |
| 10 | 2026-11-18 | Premium passes are **advantage, not immunity**: G-Suit Pro +3G and no G-LOC (shake kept); afterburner burn −60%; multi-lock locks 4 targets with +25% turn, still flare-able. | Dev | Preserves the must-keep G and fuel tension and keeps PvP winnable for free players. |
| 11 | 2026-11-18 | Original systems added: Bank It (Sortie Cash), Perfect Launch, Nerve Meter, Revenge Mark, Formation Bonus, Storm Cells, Ace Evade, Giant Slayer. | BCDK | Developer asked for original, creative solutions; each reinforces a hero beat (see GDD §9). |
| 12 | 2026-11-18 | All 3D art (jets, carrier, ships, AI aircraft, environment kit, missiles, props) is modelled in Blender via MCP; no primitive-part placeholders in the shipped game. | Dev | Developer instruction. |
| 13 | 2026-11-18 | Steering changed from "cursor offset = turn" to **camera-led aim**: input rotates a world-space aim direction, the camera follows it and the jet chases it; release = fly straight where you pointed. Mouse locks to centre in flight. | BCDK | Playtest showed offset steering always kept turning (loops). Camera-led aim is stable and beginner-friendly on touch, mouse and pad. |
| 14 | 2026-11-18 | Climb-out assist: after launch the jet climbs to ~280 studs then levels unless the pilot steers. | BCDK | Rookies otherwise climb forever or drift into the sea right after launch. |
| 15 | 2026-11-18 | Blender assets are uploaded to the **Blue Chip Games** group via Studio's Open Cloud MCP and loaded at runtime by asset id from `Config/` (InsertService). Part colours/materials come from config (paint schemes). | BCDK | Repo stays the source of truth; group ownership keeps assets usable in the group game; liveries are data. |
| 16 | 2026-11-18 | Studio-only QA hooks: `ServerStorage.AS_Debug` BindableFunction, `TestAim` / `QA_Lock` player attributes. Never created in live servers (`RunService:IsStudio()` guard). | BCDK | Automated playtests can't reach live module instances or a real mouse. |
| 17 | 2026-11-18 | Audio is a headline feature (developer emphasis). 25 ElevenLabs SFX + 6 radio VO lines ("Jerry B. - Military Commander"). Threat-intensity mix: siren tempo/volume follow missile distance, heartbeat + G-breathing + tension drone swell, world bus ducked and low-passed ("tunnel vision"), relief whoosh + VO on evade. Thrust = ignition whump + roar + sub-bass + speed-scaled wind; weapons get thump/launch/"Fox two!", spin-down tails, kill stinger + "Splash one!". Compressor on the SFX bus. | Dev + BCDK | Developer asked for maximum tension and excitement; layered, state-driven audio gives that without clutter. |
| 18 | 2026-11-18 | 7 game passes + 6 developer products created on the universe via Open Cloud (`Tools/CreateProducts.ps1`, IDs in `Config/MonetizationConfig.luau`), for sale, with AeroStrike-styled icons. "Cash Crate L" (3733032963) was created broken by an API error and can't be updated: replaced by "Cash Crate XL" (3733033145); the broken one has no price and should be archived in the dashboard. | BCDK | Brief's product list; studio-consistent storefront. |
| 19 | 2026-11-18 | Receipts are idempotent (PurchaseId recorded in the profile with the grant); Starter Ace Bundle is once per account (a repeat purchase converts to Cash so nobody loses Robux); offer shown after the 3rd shoot-down, once per session. | BCDK | Monetisation standard; fair offers. |
| 20 | 2026-11-18 | Rhino HX (Twin Lock) and Wraith-X (Low Observable, Robux or Rank 15 + 60k Cash) built in Blender; real multi-target locking for Twin Lock / Multi-Lock pass (salvo warheads 40 dmg, still flare-able). | BCDK | GDD scope (4 jets); premium stays earnable. |
| 21 | 2026-11-18 | Ace Bounty (5-kill streak, 500→2,000, Giant Slayer +50%), Wingman squads (invite/accept, max 4, Formation refuel), daily track (100→1,500 over 7 days; shown on the deck, never during the tutorial or a fight). | BCDK | GDD §9, §18, §21. |
| 9 | 2026-11-18 | FTUE is played in the live map (a private training lane right after the first catapult launch), not in a separate place. | BCDK | Keeps "zero lobbies"; every new player sees the carrier and live combat within 5 s. |
| 22 | 2026-11-18 | Mission rotation: Bomber Squadron → Destroyer Strike → Canyon Run. **Destroyer Strike:** a Blender destroyer sails a looping route with radar-aimed flak (it threatens without one-shotting); everyone who damages it shares the sink reward; when sunk it lists and slides under over about 6s. **Canyon Run:** 11 glowing gates along the volcanic canyon under the arches, 4 SAM sites, a gold time of 32s for a bonus, and it can be repeated while the mission is live. | BCDK | GDD scope (3 mission types). One co-op "big target" beat and one solo skill/time-trial beat alongside the bomber dogfight. |
| 23 | 2026-11-18 | QA verified by autopilot: Canyon Run 11/11 gates at 27.8s (gold) with no arch collisions and the reward paid; SAMs engage in the canyon; flak hits a circling jet (about 30 HP over 12s); the sink pays participants and ends the mission. | BCDK | Definition of Done for milestone 4. |
| 24 | 2026-11-18 | **Audio de-shrill pass** (developer: "some sounds sound like a kettle"). I measured every sound's spectrum. The missile warning was a pure 4 kHz tone, sped up to 1.7x near impact (about 7 kHz). Changes: (a) cockpit tones are now synthesised (warm 620/780 Hz two-tone with a sub-octave and soft edges; enemy lock 520 Hz; our lock 900 Hz with a gentle warble), and urgency comes from crossfading to a fast-beep layer, never from pitch; (b) the afterburner, cannon spin-down, reward, hits, flares, evade and missile launch were regenerated with deep, no-whistle prompts, and the best of 4 takes was picked by measurement; (c) a de-whistle DSP pass notches narrow tonal spikes and adds a gentle roll-off above 9 kHz; (d) engine and wind pitch rise is capped; (e) a -3 dB high-shelf on both buses. Sources: `Assets/Audio/v2/`. | Dev + BCDK | Audio is a headline feature; tension should come from rhythm and weight, not harsh frequencies. |
| 25 | 2026-11-18 | Crash kill-credit only counts if an enemy hit you in the last 10s (`CrashCreditWindow`). Self-kills never grant streaks. Accepting an invite to a full squad no longer removes you from your current one. | BCDK | Multiplayer review: stale `LastAttacker` let one old hit claim a bounty minutes later. |
| 26 | 2026-11-18 | Responsive UI (`ResponsiveUIController`): a UIScale on every fixed-size panel (screen height / 720, clamped 0.58 to 1.15). On touch devices the readout columns move in toward the reticle; the ammo panel sits above the thumb arc; deck buttons form one scaling row. Gamepad: Y = Hangar, X = Shop, D-pad up = Squad, B = close, with focus on menu open and glyph labels. Roblox's default touch controls are disabled. A layout check finds no clipping or overlaps at 667x375, 844x390, 1024x768, 1180x820, 1280x720, 1366x768, 1920x1080 and 2560x1440. | BCDK | Mobile-first brief; console players couldn't reach the Hangar or Shop from the deck. |
