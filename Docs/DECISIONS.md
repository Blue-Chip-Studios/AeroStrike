| 22 | 2026-11-18 | Mission rotation: Bomber Squadron → Destroyer Strike → Canyon Run. **Destroyer Strike:** a Blender destroyer sails a looping route with radar-aimed flak (it threatens without one-shotting); everyone who damages it shares the sink reward; when sunk it lists and slides under over about 6s. **Canyon Run:** 11 glowing gates along the volcanic canyon under the arches, 4 SAM sites, a gold time of 32s for a bonus, and it can be repeated while the mission is live. | BCDK | GDD scope (3 mission types). | 23 | 2026-11-18 | QA verified by autopilot: Canyon Run 11/11 gates at 27.8s (gold) with no arch collisions and the reward paid; SAMs engage in the canyon; flak hits a circling jet (about 30 HP over 12s); the sink pays participants and ends the mission. | BCDK | Definition of Done for milestone 4. |
| 24 | 2026-11-18 | **Audio de-shrill pass** (developer: "some sounds sound like a kettle"). I measured every sound's spectrum. The missile warning was a pure 4 kHz tone, sped up to 1.7x near impact (about 7 kHz). Changes: (a) cockpit tones are now synthesised (warm 620/780 Hz two-tone with a sub-octave and soft edges; enemy lock 520 Hz; our lock 900 Hz with a gentle warble), and urgency comes from crossfading to a fast-beep layer, never from pitch; (b) the afterburner, cannon spin-down, reward, hits, flares, evade and missile launch were regenerated with deep, no-whistle prompts, and the best of 4 takes was picked by measurement; (c) a de-whistle DSP pass notches narrow tonal spikes and adds a gentle roll-off above 9 kHz; (d) engine and wind pitch rise is capped; (e) a -3 dB high-shelf on both buses. Sources: `Assets/Audio/v2/`. | Dev + BCDK | Audio is a headline feature; tension should come from rhythm and weight, not harsh frequencies. |
| 25 | 2026-11-18 | Crash kill-credit only counts if an enemy hit you in the last 10s (`CrashCreditWindow`). Self-kills never grant streaks. Accepting an invite to a full squad no longer removes you from your current one. | BCDK | Multiplayer review: stale `LastAttacker` let one old hit claim a bounty minutes later. |
| 26 | 2026-11-18 | Responsive UI (`ResponsiveUIController`): a UIScale on every fixed-size panel (screen height / 720, clamped 0.58 to 1.15). On touch devices the readout columns move in toward the reticle; the ammo panel sits above the thumb arc; deck buttons form one scaling row. Gamepad: Y = Hangar, X = Shop, D-pad up = Squad, B = close, with focus on menu open and glyph labels. Roblox's default touch controls are disabled. A layout check finds no clipping or overlaps at 667x375, 844x390, 1024x768, 1180x820, 1280x720, 1366x768, 1920x1080 and 2560x1440. | BCDK | Mobile-first brief; console players couldn't reach the Hangar or Shop from the deck. |
| 27 | 2026-11-18 | **Live 2-player test** (Studio local server): PvP cannon kill credited ("SPLASH! +150", kill feed); spawn-shielded attackers can't hurt players; 5-kill streak made Player1 an Ace (◉750, announced to all); Player2 claimed it (+750) and it cleared; squad invite/accept works and squadmates take zero gun damage and can't be targeted by missiles. Added from the test: **Ace marker** (gold "★ ACE ◉value · km" billboard on other players' Aces, through terrain; contrails alone were only visible up close). | BCDK | Brief: "high-value Ace Bounty marker so underdogs hunt whales". |
| 28 | 2026-11-18 | **Launch safety + PULL UP.** For 4s after the catapult (below 100 studs) the aim can't drop below a 6° climb, so one stray flick off the bow (about 30 studs above the sea) can't kill a new pilot. One-frame mouse jumps over 250px (focus/alt-tab) are ignored. A ground-proximity **PULL UP** cue (flashing text plus a synthesised 440 Hz double-beep) warns about 3s before the flight path meets sea or terrain. Verified: no false alarm on the climb-out; 3.0s of warning in a dive. | BCDK | The test showed a player crashing into the sea twice right after launch; the first 10 minutes must be flawless. |
| 29 | 2026-11-18 | Second 2-player run. **Ace marker verified** (the hunter sees "★ ACE ◉750" with live distance, attached to the Ace's jet, always on top; the Ace sees none on themself). **Bug fixed:** chased-crash kill credit never fired, because client-reported crashes arrive as `crash:<hit>` and the server only matched the exact `crash`. | 34 | 2026-11-18 | **Cannon audio is per round** (developer: holding fire played one sample; wanted every bullet audible and realistic). Each round plays a 30mm revolver-cannon shot from a 10-voice pool, alternating 2 variants with pitch/volume jitter; a feed-mechanism loop runs underneath while firing; spin-down on release. Verified 27 shots for 27 rounds over 1.5s. | Dev | Rhythm locked to the gun; realistic weight. |
| 35 | 2026-11-18 | **Heat-seeker lock difficulty** (developer: locks need difficulty tied to a mechanic). Lock rate = aspect (1.6x tail, 0.35x head-on) x range x boresight x G (seeker shakes above 4G) x target type (Drone 0.75, Bomber 1.15, Interceptor 1.25, stealth 1.4) x evading; terrain between you blocks it, ground clutter halves it, the sun blinds it; target flares knock 55% off; out-of-cone progress bleeds away. HUD coaching (GET ON THEIR SIX / TERRAIN MASKING / LOST IN THE SUN / FLARES! RE-ACQUIRE). The server only accepts a missile when that player announced a full lock on the primary target. | Dev + BCDK | Skill = getting on their six; can't be bypassed client-side. |
| 36 | 2026-11-18 | **AI tactics + Red Talon interceptor** (developer ideas: hug terrain, go vertical with flares, dogfight). Tracked AI picks a tactic after a reaction delay: Jink, Mask (dive to the lowest ground or the canyon, 60-70 studs AGL), Vertical (climb with two flare salvos), Break (turn into the attacker). Each counters a lock factor. New Interceptor (Viper airframe in Talon charcoal with PBR skin, 150 speed, agile, pursues players, 5 rps nose guns about 2.6 dps) escorts bomber squadrons; kill = 120 cash / 80 XP. All AI gets terrain avoidance. Verified: masked within 0.5s, went vertical with flares that broke the lock, and out-turned a naive autopilot. | Dev + BCDK | Dogfights with real decisions; terrain becomes tactical. |
| 37 | 2026-11-18 | **Kill payoff** (developer: "is the payoff good enough?"). A charred copy of the downed aircraft tumbles from the sky with fire, a smoke column and burning debris, then bursts or splashes on impact. A centre banner SPLASH! escalates to DOUBLE/TRIPLE/ACE SPLASH within 6s, with a camera kick. Plus the existing explosion, stinger, "Splash one!" VO and reward toast. The death card names the AI/hazard killer (RED TALON, SAM, TAIL GUNNER) instead of "The ground". | BCDK | The moment of victory should be the most satisfying beat in the game. |
| 38 | 2026-11-18 | **Missiles always leave the rail** (playtest: "couldn't fire a missile"). Without a full lock, the missile fires **unguided** (flies straight for 4.5s, proximity fuse 1.8x) and the HUD says NO LOCK - UNGUIDED. The lock box shows SEEKING n% while acquiring. The lock rate has a floor (0.3x) so stacked factors can't make a lock impossible; tuned head-on, range, boresight and decay. Verified: a no-lock shot leaves the rail (2→1, 1 in flight); a tail-on lock in 0.7s fires a guided missile at the target. | Dev + BCDK | Pressing fire must always do something; the guided shot stays the skill reward. |
| 39 | 2026-11-18 | **Throttle + energy** (playtest: always full speed, felt too easy). Persistent throttle: W/S (RT/LT) ramp it; holding 100% for 0.3s, or Shift, lights the afterburner; mouse wheel ±10%; touch slider on the left edge. Speed runs from idle to military power, with the afterburner on top. Hard turns bleed speed (above 2.5G); roll-in authority; a **stall** below min speed (nose drops, controls soften). Fuel burn scales with throttle. HUD THR gauge + STALL warning. Verified 100% = 662 km/h, 40% = 416, 0% = 251. | Dev + BCDK | Speed is a decision, not a constant; turning costs energy, so flying well is a skill. |
| 40 | 2026-11-18 | **Refuel only by tanker or carrier** (playtest: "randomly refuelled mid-flight"). Removed the tutorial gold fuel ring and the squad formation fuel trickle. A Blender KC-tanker (friendly, persistent streaming) orbits inside the map with a green TANKER marker. Fly into the zone behind the drogue, match speed (±35) and fuel flows at 18/s; once hooked, the jet holds the tanker's speed. Below 30% fuel the marker turns amber and becomes the objective (LOW FUEL prompt). Carrier touch-and-go still refuels. Verified: refuel 21→34% in 0.8s. | Dev | Fuel tension needs deliberate refuelling; a tanker run is its own aerial beat. |
| 41 | 2026-11-18 | **Damage feedback** (playtest: did the gun do damage? can I see I'm hit?). On every aircraft: light smoke under 65% HP, fire + black smoke under 35%. Your hull: HULL bar, red hurt flash + vignette + camera kick on each hit, HULL CRITICAL with the eject prompt. Your gun hits: X hit marker + the target's HP bar. | Dev | Hits must read instantly, both dealt and taken. |
| 42 | 2026-11-18 | **Eject + parachute.** Hold X (keyboard) / Y (pad) for 0.6s, or tap EJECT on touch (shown at critical hull).
