01High concept

DEVAeroStrike is an instant-action arcade fighter-jet game. Players spawn straight onto a carrier catapult and are in the air within 5 seconds, then dogfight rival players and complete live missions that spawn in the sky. Kills and missions earn Cash and Ace XP, which unlock faster jets, better missiles, more flares, bigger fuel tanks and G-suits.

< 5 sspawn to airborne
< 60 sfirst lock-on and missile fired (FTUE)
~25 minfirst jet unlock (Viper IX)

02Player fantasy

DEV"Untouchable fighter ace": blast off the deck at Mach 1, hear the missile-lock alarm, pop flares at the last millisecond, pull heavy Gs through canyon arches and turn the tables on a rival.

BCDKThe fantasy is delivered as three "hero beats" that every session must hit within its first minute: The Launch (catapult and sonic crack), The Lock (lock tone rising to a solid tone, missile away) and The Evade (siren, flare, missile spirals away, "ACE EVADE!"). Every system is judged by whether it makes these beats louder.

03Target audience

DEVAges 9 to 18+. Mobile and tablet first, plus PC and console. BCDKTwo personas: "Rookie" (9 to 13, mobile, wants explosions and to feel good fast, low tolerance for menus) and "Ace" (13 to 18+, PC or controller, wants mastery, rank and bragging rights). Every design choice must work for the Rookie and have depth for the Ace.

05Core gameplay loop

DEVCatapult → dogfight and missions → earn Cash and Ace XP → buy jets, missiles, flares, fuel and G-suits (or Robux) → relaunch.

BCDKBank It (new): Cash from kills and missions is held as Sortie Cash until you bank it by touching down on the carrier deck (a touch-and-go also refuels and rearms) for a ×1.25 bonus. If you're shot down you keep 75% (25% is lost). This gives the carrier a reason to exist after launch, ties into the fuel tension in your brief, and creates "one more kill or go home?" decisions. Mission rewards bank automatically, so Rookies never feel robbed.

06Session loop

BCDKA typical 20-minute session is 4 to 6 sorties. Join, already on the catapult, launch. Fight and do missions for 2 to 4 minutes per sortie. Bank or get shot down. Hangar on the deck (10 to 30 s: claim the daily reward, buy an upgrade). Relaunch. A mission rotation every 3 minutes and the Ace Bounty keep the server's goals changing, so there's always a reason for one more sortie.

07FTUE: the first 10 minutes

DEV"A flawless first 10 minutes." BCDKThe FTUE is played in the live map, in a training lane (a private ring course on the carrier's lee side, protected by the carrier's air-defence umbrella), so new pilots see real players fighting in the distance from second one. No text walls: every step is one prompt plus one action, voiced by "Deck Boss" (ElevenLabs).

09Game mechanics

Flight

BCDKArcade model: speed 180 to 900 km/h (trainer) up to 1,250 km/h (stealth). Turn rate falls at very high speed, so the "corner speed" sweet spot (~600 km/h) is the skill ceiling. Stalling is impossible below the minimum speed; the jet nose-dips and recovers. Terrain collision destroys the jet unless the spawn shield is active (then it bounces).

Combat triangle DEV

  • Nose cannon: rapid fire with tracers every 3rd round, and a lead-indicator pip on a target in range (under 800 m). Overheats after ~3 s of continuous fire (heat bar), rewarding bursts.
  • Heat-seeking missiles: keep the target inside the lock circle for the lock time (1.2 s base) → rising tone → solid tone and red box "LOCK" → fire. Missiles have limited turn rate: sharp turns, terrain masking and flares defeat them.
  • Flares: each flare has a 70% decoy chance (90% within the last 0.6 s of missile flight: "Ace Evade" BCDK, a cash bonus plus a slow-motion beat). Visible flare arcs; decoyed missiles spiral and detonate on the flare.

G-force and fuel DEV

  • G meter builds with turn rate × speed. Above 6G: camera shake, a red/grey edge vignette and stiffer steering. At 9G for more than 2 s: "G-LOC" grey-out for 1.5 s (controls go soft, not lost). G-suit upgrades raise the thresholds.
  • Fuel: the base burn is slow; the afterburner burns 8× faster. At 0 fuel, top speed is capped at 55% (no flame-out: it's a kid-friendly tension, not a death). Refuel at golden fuel rings (spawn along flight corridors) or via a carrier touch-and-go.

Anti-spawn-camp DEV

The carrier zone (1.2 km radius) has automated CIWS (guns) and SAMs that only target players with kills in the last 30 s who enter the zone, with a clear warning ring first. Launched jets get a 6 s spawn shield (a visible shimmer) that breaks early if they fire.

Ace Bounty DEV

A player on a 5-kill streak becomes an Ace: a gold diamond is shown to every player on HUD and map, and a gold contrail. The bounty value rises with streak length (500 → 2,000 Cash) and is paid to whoever shoots them down. Bounty kills by a much lower-rank pilot pay +50% (Giant Slayer BCDK).

11Map: "Kestrel Archipelago"

DEVOne carrier hub plus one multi-biome island map. BCDKLayout (about 8 × 8 km of playable airspace, streamed):

  • Open ocean fleet (east): destroyer patrol routes; flat, fast, exposed.
  • Volcanic canyon trenches (island centre): narrow red-rock canyons with natural arches, lava glow, the canyon checkpoint run and Nerve farming.
  • High-altitude cloud ceiling (above 2,500 m): volumetric cloud layer, storm cells, bomber squadron lanes.
  • Carrier "Anvil" (west): spawn hub, hangar deck, CIWS/SAM umbrella, training lane on its lee side.

BCDKThe pilot avatar is only seen in the hangar and the kill card (player's own avatar in a flight suit and helmet accessory overlay). 17Rewards

Kill-card popups, "+150" Cash toasts that fly into the Sortie Cash counter, rank-up fanfare, Ace Evade slow-mo, a 7-day daily login track (100 → 1,500 Cash, day 7 = paint scheme) BCDK, and 3 daily challenges ("Ace Evade 3 times", "Sink a destroyer").

18Social systems

DEVWingman squads (2 to 4): friends in the server get a one-tap "Invite to squad" chip on spawn; squadmates can't lock each other, share mission credit and get the co-op multiplier. BCDKSquad markers (blue chevrons), Formation Bonus, and squad-spawn: launching right after a wingman uses the next catapult so you take off together.

19–20Multiplayer and matchmaking

BCDKFree-for-all, 16 players per server (performance budget for jets, missiles and AI). No matches: the server is a persistent sky with 3-minute mission rotations and a 15-minute "Top Ace" leaderboard reset that pays the top 3. Standard Roblox matchmaking fills servers; friends follow into the same server.

21Retention

Daily login track · daily challenges · rank insignia · jet mastery (per-jet kill counts unlock paint) · weekly "Top Ace" global leaderboard · rotating weekend events (e.g. "Bomber Blitz": 2× mission cash) BCDK.

22Monetisation

DEVPay-to-win and fast-track progression, with direct Robux unlocks. Free players can still earn top-tier jets.

BCDK flag: needs your decision. Two of the requested passes as written, G-Force Immunity and Unlimited Fuel / Infinite Afterburner, remove the two tension systems your brief lists as must-keeps. In a 9 to 18 PvP game, "can't be beaten" purchases also push free players (usually more than 95% of the audience) to quit, which shrinks the pool of targets payers enjoy shooting. BCDKRecommendation: keep every product and keep it powerful, but make it strong advantage, not immunity (the "Recommended" column). Every premium weapon stays counterable by flares and terrain.

25UI/UX

BCDKTwo layers: the HUD (green fighter-HUD telemetry over the world: speed and altitude tapes, lock circle, target boxes, missile warning, G meter, fuel bar, ammo) and the Deck UI (gunmetal panels with amber accents for hangar, shop and results). References in Docs/UI/References/ are used for layout and hierarchy only. AeroStrike's visual identity is its own and is defined in the UI design system (Phase 3).

  • Screens: Spawn/Launch overlay · Flight HUD · Kill card / kill cam · Death → relaunch (one tap) · Deck Hangar (jets, upgrades, paints) · Shop · Daily reward · Squad · Map · Settings (controls, sensitivity, button layout).
  • Golden rule: no screen blocks relaunch. LAUNCH is always one tap from anywhere on the deck.

26Art direction: Stylized (military aviation)

DEVSleek, mature modern military aviation: metallic PBR jet bodies, dramatic volumetric clouds, crisp tracers, glowing afterburners, clean green HUD. Never blocky or childish; 60 fps on mobile.

  • Shape language: slightly exaggerated silhouettes (bigger intakes and canopies, swept wings, readable from 2 km), soft bevels, no greebles. Each jet has a unique silhouette at icon size.
  • Materials: SurfaceAppearance with a shared 512 px trim/atlas per jet family: gunmetal, panel lines baked into the normal map, metalness and roughness for sheen. Paint schemes are colour-map swaps.
  • World: stylised terrain (Roblox Terrain + custom MaterialVariants), red volcanic rock kit, low-poly stylised pines, the ocean as Terrain water.
  • Lighting preset "Golden Sortie": Future lighting, warm low sun, Atmosphere density 0.3 with blue haze, Clouds cover 0.55, Bloom 0.6, SunRays, ColorCorrection contrast +0.08.
  • Palette: HUD green #5CFF8F · Amber #FFC23D · Threat red #FF4B3E · Squad blue #4FA9FF · Gunmetal #131D28.
  • All 3D art is made in Blender via MCP DEV: four jets, carrier, destroyer, bomber, drones, missiles, flares, SAM site, canyon rock and arch kit, pines, rings. A style kit is built first and shown for approval.

Authority: the client owns its jet's physics for responsiveness. The server checks max speed per jet, teleport distance, fire rate, ammo, lock geometry and hit claims (range, angle, line of sight), and owns damage, kills, Cash, XP and purchases. Missiles are server-simulated at 20 Hz and replicated as compact state; clients interpolate the visuals. Remotes are rate-limited; payloads are buffer-serialised via BCDK Remote.

30Performance

StreamingEnabled; jets 3 to 6k tris with an LOD at 300 m; pooled tracers, flares, explosions and missiles; cosmetic VFX client-only; 16-player cap; AI under 12 units; mobile graphics fallback (no SunRays, lower cloud density). Budget: 60 fps on iPhone 12 / mid Android at 16 players.

31Platforms

Touch first (safe areas via ScreenInsets, movable buttons), full controller navigation for every menu (Selectable, NextSelection*), keyboard and mouse. Text 14 pt minimum on phone. Haptics on missile warning (mobile and controller).
