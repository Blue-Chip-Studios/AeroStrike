## Settings to check before going public

- **Maturity & Compliance questionnaire:** currently rates the game **13+**. Arcade jets, explosions, no blood or gore normally answer as *mild violence* (9+). Redo it honestly; 9+ opens the game to under-13 players, a large share of Roblox players and of your ad reach.
- **VR:** enabled, but the game has no VR controls. Turn it off so VR players don't land in a broken experience.
- **Genre:** Action (Roblox may refine it automatically).
- **Private servers:** enable at a low price (friend groups).
- **Automatic translation:** turn on (Localization).
