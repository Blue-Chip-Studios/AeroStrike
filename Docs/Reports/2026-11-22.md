# AeroStrike daily metrics – Sun 22 Nov 2026

Data: Fri 20 – Sun 22 Nov (UTC). Today is a partial day (583 players, 629 sessions) but **every session today ran v113**, so it is the cleanest read on the new changes. Saturday mixed old (v105) and new builds.

## Headline

The v111–v113 rookie changes worked on what they targeted: new players now get a first kill far more often (62%, was 26% on Friday), crash/eject deaths are down from 64% to 50% of rookie deaths, and the sonic-boom error is gone. **But players are not staying longer**: average session is flat at 3.6 min (baseline 3.8, target 10+), and there are still no purchases. The biggest new finding is that the most common moment to leave is right after the first kill.

## What worked and what didn't

**Worked**
- **Rookie bandit / easy first kill**: shown to 331 players today; new-player first kill rose to 62% (26% Friday).
- **Air-start redeploy (LAUNCH INTO THE FIGHT)**: used for 51% of all sorties today (1,089 air-starts by 227 players). Players clearly like it.
- **Rookie survival (hull repair, auto pull-up)**: rookie ejects 32% → 22%, terrain/sea crashes 24% → 19%. Shot down by enemies is now the main way rookies die (51%), which is the healthy outcome.
- **Controller remap + aim assist**: controller players in the tutorial reaching the guns step 22% → 44%; controller first kill 12% → 37%.
- **Free rookie revive**: used by 99 players today.
- **Notification prompt before favourite**: 168 notification prompts today vs ~6/day before (favourite prompts 80).
- **Sonic-boom error fix**: 0 errors in v113; all 471 on Saturday came from older builds.

**Didn't work (yet)**
- **Session length is flat** (3.6 min) despite the much easier first kill. Of today's new-player sessions, **38% ended with the first kill as the last thing they did** (223 of 585). Players get their kill and leave.
- **Controller players still play only 2.0 min** on average (keyboard 5.1, touch 3.7), and they are a quarter of new players (137 of 547 today). 36 of 54 controller players in the tutorial abandoned it and 13 skipped it.
- **Hard landings unchanged** at 8% of rookie deaths, even with landing challenges removed for rookies.
- **Hangar nudges**: hangar share of new players went down slightly (15% → 13%). Today's opens by source: deck 64, death-screen link 52, death button 23, death nudge only 4.
- **Shop**: 87 Revive Here and 29 Starter Ace purchase prompts today, 0 bought (3 days: 300 prompts, 0 purchases). The free rookie revive may be taking the place of the paid revive.

## Performance and mid-flight quits (v112+ data)

- **Loading is fine**: 98%+ of sessions load in under 10 s on every device.
- **Frame rate is mostly fine**: touch averages 48 fps (11% of touch sessions stutter), keyboard 93, controller 58. Sessions under 20 fps are shorter (3.5–4.7 min vs ~5.6 min at 45+ fps), but they are only ~3% of sessions. Low frame rate is not the main reason players leave.
- **Mid-flight quits (890 players)**: 69% had been in a fight within the last 10 s, 82% had an enemy within 2 km, and 80% had a healthy hull. Only ~11% had gone 30 s+ without a fight. **Players are not leaving because they can't find action, so an "action finder" arrow is not recommended.** They leave in the middle of the action, usually right after a kill (see above).

## Top 3 recommendations for the next build

1. **Give players a clear next goal the moment they get their first kill.** 38% of new players leave right after it, and first kills are now 62% but sessions have not moved. Right after the kill, show "2 more kills to rank 2" plus progress toward the Viper, and auto-invite them to the Bomber Squadron mission (only 0.06 missions finished per session today). This is the most direct lever on session length and D1.
2. **Fix the controller experience.** Controller players average 2.0 min vs 5.1 on keyboard and make up 25% of new players. 36 of 54 controller tutorial players abandoned it. Watch a controller playtest of the first 2 minutes, add on-screen RT/LT button hints in the tutorial, and check camera/aim feel. Bringing them to the touch average (3.7 min) alone would lift overall average by ~0.4 min.
3. **Get the first purchase.** 300 purchase prompts, 0 buys in 3 days. Revive Here gets most of the prompts (87 today), but rookies now revive free. Add a low-price one-time starter offer (around 25–49 R$) shown after the first rank-up, and make the paid revive clearly better than the free one.

Quick win alongside these: switch all players to the tutorial (A/B verdict above, worth about +0.4 min per new player).
