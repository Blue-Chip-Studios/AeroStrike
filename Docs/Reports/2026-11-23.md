## Headline

v118 is a small step forward. More players are still playing at 5 minutes (22.6%, up from 19.6% yesterday and 21% at baseline), fewer leave right after a death (40%, baseline 49%), and the new "NEXT GOAL" line seems to keep players going after their first kill. **But average session is still 3.8 min (target 10+), there are still no purchases, and the controller fix did not help: controller players still play only 2.3 min.** Two new problems: the tutorial now blocks the "easy first kill" rookie bot for most new players, and last night's first notification send crashed partway, so we have no send numbers yet.

## What worked and what didn't (22 Nov batch, v118)

**Worked**
- **NEXT GOAL line after the first kill.** Of new players who left, the share whose last milestone was their first kill fell from **30% yesterday (419 of 1,394) to 19% today (68 of 364)**. Of players who got a first kill, about 38% stopped there today vs 57% yesterday. This was the biggest leak in the last report and it is shrinking.
- **Everyone gets the tutorial.** No "direct" players today, and new-player 5-minute survival is 22.5% (yesterday 18.7%), in line with the Tutorial group from the test. No sign of a problem from switching everyone over.
- **Fewer quits right after death**: 40.1% of new players today (Sat 49%, Sun 41%).
- **Starter Ace from rank 2 at 49 Robux** is reaching more people: viewed by 47 players today (12% of players) vs 107 yesterday (8%) and 95 on Saturday (6%).

**Didn't work (yet)**
- **Controller death-screen fix (A = launch, Y = hangar)**: controller sorties per session are **1.3 today** (1.4 yesterday, 1.0 on Saturday), and only **5 hangar opens** came from the pad button. The death screen is not where controller players are lost: most of them never get that far. Of 55 new controller players today, 13 skipped the tutorial, 34 abandoned it, only **12 (22%) reached the guns step** and only **9 (16%) got a first kill** (keyboard 83%, touch 47% over 3 days). Controller players are 18% of all players.
- **Rookie bot is now hidden behind the tutorial (side effect).** The "easy first kill" rookie bot does not spawn while a player is in training (`Modules/Server/FTUEServer.luau:141`). Now that everyone gets the tutorial, it reached **only 23 of 351 new players today (7%) vs 403 of 1,280 yesterday (31%)**. New-player first kill fell from 57% to 51%.
- **Rookie missiles reloading out of combat**: hard landings are **8.1% of rookie deaths every day** (Sat, Sun and Mon), no change. Ejects went up to 28% (small sample: 492 rookie deaths).
- **Shop**: 3 days, **324 purchase prompts, 0 purchases**. Today: Starter Ace 47 prompts / 35 cancelled, Revive Here 45 / 30 cancelled. About 1 in 4 prompts has no result logged at all (no purchase, no cancel), so it is worth checking the Creator Dashboard sales page once to confirm there really were no sales.
- **Hangar**: 11% of new players open it (target 30%+). Most opens come from the deck (47) and the death-screen link (26).

## Top 3 recommendations for the next build

1. **Bring back the rookie first-kill bot for tutorial players.** It reached only 7% of new players on v118 (31% before), and new-player first kill fell from 57% to 51%. Spawn it the moment a player finishes or leaves the tutorial (about two thirds of new players, 236 of 351 today, leave the tutorial partway). The first kill is what the NEXT GOAL line builds on, so this should lift both first kills and session length. Small change, high impact.
2. **Fix controller onboarding: the first 2 minutes, not the death screen.** Controller players average 2.3 min (touch 3.8, keyboard 5.9) and only 22% reach the guns step and 16% get a first kill. Make the tutorial's fire/lock steps controller-specific (big on-screen RT/LT/button icons, auto-advance on any trigger press), and look at why 13 of 55 skip it. Getting controller players to the touch average would add about 0.3 min to the overall average session.
3. **Get the first purchase.** 324 prompts and 0 buys in 3 days, even at 49 Robux. First confirm in the Creator Dashboard that no sales are missing from our tracking (1 in 4 prompts has no result logged). Then try a cheaper first purchase of real value at the moment players feel it, such as a 25 Robux "Revive + 2x cash for this life" on the death screen (Revive Here already gets 45 prompts a day), since rookies currently revive for free.

**Before tonight (not a game build):** make `Tools/send_daily_notifications.py` skip and count a player whose request times out, instead of crashing, so tonight's send finishes, prints its totals and gets logged. Without it we can't measure notifications, which are the main lever for D1 (4.2% now, target 10–15%).
