AEROSTRIKE  ·  UI DESIGN SYSTEM v1

Two layers: HUD (green telemetry drawn over the world, transparent, glows) and Deck (gunmetal panels for hangar, shop and modals). The signature shape is the chamfered corner (45° cuts top-left and bottom-right); the signature accent is runway chevrons . References were used for layout and information hierarchy only.
